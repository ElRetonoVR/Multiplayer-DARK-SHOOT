THE BLOOM
Origin of THE BLOOM
Years ago, a microscopic life form was discovered that was capable of:
adhering to human tissue
replicating genetic information
learning from its host
The experiment sought to create adaptable organisms for extreme survival.
It failed.
The parasite not only invaded the body... it invaded the environment
Infected people did not die immediately.
Their bodies became incubators, generating new incomplete forms.
The Bloom is:
the result of multiple fused infections
a ‘central node’
a creature that no longer needs a host... it seeks them out

THE BLOOM
Physical Description:
Height: ~1 metre
Type: Parasitic entity / biological invader
Movement: Clumsy but persistent
The creature has an aberrant, compact and deformed anatomy, as if several
incomplete organisms had been fused into one. Its body appears unfinished, with
irregular proportions and a hunched posture that reinforces its unnatural character.
Its skin is pale, moist and organic, covered with protuberances, exposed veins and
small embedded humanoid forms, suggesting failed attempts at replication. These
forms resemble ‘alien babies’ trapped at different stages of development, some still
with visible facial expressions.
The main head is disproportionately large, with deformed infantile features:
Sunken, glassy eyes, always open
A mouth too large for its face
An elongated, flexible tongue, used for both attacking and exploring
Organic branches, similar to roots or tentacles, emerge from the back of its head and
back, extending throughout the environment. These branches are not only part of its
body, but also its propagation system.
Its hands are large, with long fingers and deformed claws, designed more for
crawling and grasping than for quick attacks.

THE BLOOM
BEHAVIOUR / AI
The enemy does not run, but never stops, slowly pursuing you, forcing you to:
hide
advance cautiously
MAIN ATTACK (KEY TO TERROR)
When the enemy catches up with the player:
The screen darkens
Ambient sound is cut off
A large, sudden, and very close image of its face appears
eyes wide open
distorted mouth
wet sound / breathing
It's not just a jumpscare:
It's the feeling that you're already within its reach, that it has already ‘touched’ you.

BRANCHES
BRANCHES (MECHANICS + ENVIRONMENT)
The branches:
Grow on walls, ceilings, and floors.
Are organic, pulsating, dark.
Are not just decoration:
They reduce visibility.
They increase tension in the dark.
In advanced levels, the ramifications:
Block routes
Create ‘living zones’
Can alert the enemy if shot or stepped
on
They will react to the player's sound and
movement in more advanced levels or
with greater game development, but not
in the first instalment.

STORY
It happens in an Apocaliptycal environment.
As we wrote a group of scientist discovered a new life form with a particular adaptive
system and they made experimental mutations with humans to extend life. It failed.
So the group of CDC (Center for desease control) goes to an underground lab
looking a weapon capable to destroy the threat. Now they must fight their old
partners and survive to ensure the survivability of all humankind.
There they found a bunch of Blooms defending the weapon. So, they should fight
with them, especifically shoot them. Also, we should remember that the city has been
overriden by blooms, so, they are in danger all the time.
While the lab was hidden underground, the bloom has already taken part of the lab, a
few walls are now already infected, some have lost their light and a very few remain
intact.
The fussion happens when the bloom arrives to human body, it infects the human
body and create the monstruosity. We could see different models of the bloom,
some of them will have labcoat, engineers, laboratorist, etc.
